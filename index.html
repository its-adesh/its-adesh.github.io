Featured project

Bank Telemarketing Analytics

Python · Tableau · Customer segmentation

Who is likely to subscribe?

I analysed 45,211 bank marketing records to explore customer groups and predict term-deposit subscriptions. I compared models and built an interactive dashboard to help explain the results.

22.7% subscription rate in Cluster 0

The highest-rate group represented 13.9% of records. The largest group had a lower subscription rate of 8.8%.

Giving subscribers more weight during model training improved recall, but also increased incorrect positive predictions. My recommendation is to test targeting against campaign costs and calling capacity.
