Featured analytics project

Bank Telemarketing Analytics

Python · Tableau · Customer segmentation

Who is likely to subscribe?

I analysed 45,211 bank marketing records to explore customer groups and predict term-deposit subscriptions. I compared models and built an interactive dashboard to help explain the results.

22.7% subscription rate in Cluster 0

The highest-rate group represented 13.9% of records. The largest group had a lower subscription rate of 8.8%.

Giving subscribers more weight during model training improved recall, but also increased incorrect positive predictions. My recommendation is to test targeting against campaign costs and calling capacity.

E-commerce analytics project

Olist E-Commerce Analytics

PostgreSQL · Power BI · DAX

What shapes sales and the delivery experience?

I analysed the Brazilian Olist dataset using SQL and built three Power BI dashboard pages covering sales performance, customer behaviour, and delivery operations.

R$15.42 million in payments from delivered orders

The dataset includes 96,478 delivered orders. Payment totals cover the 96,477 delivered orders with payment records.

My analysis explored repeat purchasing and the relationship between delivery delays and customer review scores. I recommended investigating delivery bottlenecks, prioritising regions by both delay rate and order volume, and testing proactive customer communication.

Business analysis project

Loan Approval Process Improvement

Lucidchart · As-Is / To-Be · Swimlane mapping

Improving document checks and follow-up

I mapped an assumed current loan application process and designed a proposed workflow with earlier attachment checks, clear follow-up ownership, and defined document rework loops.

Continue permitted work while documents are outstanding

The proposed process allows assessment tasks permitted by approved business rules to continue, while tasks requiring missing documents pause. Final approval remains with an authorised manager.

The To-Be map separates responsibilities across the Customer, Application System, Loan Officer, Credit Assessment, and Authorised Manager lanes.

This is a practice scenario. The assumed current process and proposed changes require stakeholder validation, and expected benefits have not been measured.
